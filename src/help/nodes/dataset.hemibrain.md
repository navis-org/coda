## About this dataset

The hemibrain covers approximately one hemisphere of the central brain of an adult female *Drosophila*, plus parts of the right optic lobe. Coda reads it from neuPrint. Neurons come with rich annotations: cell type, class, cell body fibre, soma radius, hemilineage and more.

> [!WARNING] Neurons can be truncated
> The volume does not contain the whole brain, so some neurons are cut off at the midline or at the
> edge towards the optic lobe. Keep this in mind when you compare e.g. synapse counts or cable
> length between neurons.
